# 充电上限 3.2.2

这一版修正界面细节：英文界面的时间改用 12 小时制，电量曲线按页面剩下的高度伸缩，首次引导页上下居中。

## 变化

- **英文时间用 12 小时制**（9:00 PM），和时间选择器一致。
- **电量曲线填满剩下的高度。** 内容放得下的页面不再滚动，曲线占满剩下的空间，一直充满的页面不再留白；内容放不下时照常滚动。
- **引导页居中，长问题换行。**
- **动画。** 圆盘里的百分比滚动变化；状态文字淡入淡出；今晚的计划出现和消失有过渡。

## 升级

直接覆盖安装即可，设置保留。覆盖安装 DMG 后，放电帮手要在「设置 → 高级」里重新装一次；PKG 会自己换掉它。

---

## English

Charge Limit 3.2.2 fixes a few interface details. Times in English now use a 12-hour clock (9:00 PM), matching the time pickers. Pages that fit in the window no longer scroll, and the charge curve fills the space left on them, so the always-full page no longer has a blank area. Pages that don't fit still scroll. The first-run guide is centred vertically, and its long question wraps instead of being cut off. The percentage in the disc rolls when it changes, status text cross-fades, and tonight's plan animates in and out.

## Upgrading

Install over the existing app and your settings are kept. If you install the DMG over the old app, the discharge helper stays on the old version. Reinstall it in Settings → Advanced. The PKG replaces it automatically.
